# Treatment — «أطأ بعرجتي هذه في الجنة» (عمرو بن الجموح)

**Style:** Lemo-Opuscar — Gilded Illumination (`gilded-illumination`)  
**Engine:** Remotion (React + deterministic Canvas 2D renderer + mastered audio)  
**Format:** 1080 × 1920 (9:16 Vertical Short), 30 fps, ~58–65 s  
**Language:** Egyptian Arabic narration & burned-in parchment-scroll subtitles (+ Quranic verse & Hadith quotes in classical vowelled Arabic)

---

## 1. Three Candidate Structures (and Choice)

1. **Candidate A — Framed Bestiary / Holy Cards:** Six separate gilded cards (The Verse, The Cane, The Four Lions, The Sword, The Battlefield, The Footsteps) displayed one by one in a static border. *Rejected:* too static for a dramatic emotional arc of defiance and courage.
2. **Candidate B — Continuous Vertical Tapestry (One Illuminated Scroll Ascending from Earth to Paradise):** The whole short takes place inside ONE tall vertical gilded miniature painting. The camera starts on an illuminated Quranic medallion on deep ultramarine, drops down into the quiet Medina courtyard, pulls up along the stepped spires of Mount Uhud as Amr limps through a storm of spears and dark-gold clouds, and finally ascends through a gilded arch into a starry Paradise garden where the uneven footsteps transform into straight golden steps — ending on a full vertical pull-back revealing the entire story as one living illuminated manuscript inside its border.
3. **Candidate C — Split-Screen Before/After:** Left side shows his four sons urging him to stay; right side shows the battlefield. *Rejected:* splits a narrow 9:16 vertical frame into cramped columns.

**Chosen Structure:** **Candidate B — Continuous Vertical Tapestry Ascending from Earth to Paradise**, using authentic *Gilded Illumination* grammar (flat mineral pigments, firm iron-gall ink outlines, white-lead pearls/dots, tiled burnished gold leaf that flashes ONLY when light moves across it, deep ultramarine skies that stay deep while gold carries the brightness).

---

## 2. Logline & Emotional Arc

- **Logline:** Excused from battle by a Quranic verse because of a severe limp, elderly companion Amr ibn al-Jamuh defies his four sons to fight at Uhud, hoping to limp his way straight into Paradise.
- **Arc:**
  - **Hook (0–4.5 s):** A dark gilded Quranic shamsa (medallion) ignites from a single spark — *"The Quran revealed a whole verse excusing him from war… yet he refused to stay home!"*
  - **Setup (4.5–17 s):** Deep ultramarine sky over Medina's domes and Mount Uhud's stepped spires. Amr ibn al-Jamuh leans on his staff beside his four tall, lion-hearted sons armed with shields and banners.
  - **Conflict (17–32 s):** The four sons form a protective barrier in front of the arched doorway, raising the parchment scroll of *(لَيْسَ عَلَى الْأَعْرَجِ حَرَجٌ)*. Amr's posture surges with vermilion resolve; before a radiant descending golden arch (representing the Prophet's ﷺ presence respectfully without figurative depiction), Amr's immortal plea rings out — *"والله إني لأرجو أن أطأ بعرجتي هذه في الجنة!"*
  - **Turn & Climax (32–48 s):** Near-silence, then a warm golden light beam sweeps down from the upper shamsa — *(دَعُوهُ، لَعَلَّ اللَّهَ أَنْ يَرْزُقَهُ الشَّهَادَة)*. Native move **The Touch**: golden rings spread across the dark leaf squares of Mount Uhud; Amr strides into the crimson-and-lapis clash of spears and raised shields, his sword catching sparks of gold until a calm shaft of light descends at martyrdom.
  - **Resolution & Echo (48–62 s):** Above the quiet spires of Uhud, a second golden light beam illuminates a starry ultramarine garden arch: uneven limping footprints in dark earth transform into steady, whole footprints of burnished gold walking into light — *(كَأَنِّي أَنْظُرُ إِلَيْكَ تَمْشِي بِرِجْلِكَ هَذِهِ صَحِيحَةً فِي الْجَنَّة)*. Final pull-back reveals the complete illuminated vertical miniature inside its ornate gold-and-vermilion border, with the closing aphorism *"الأعذار بتصنعها العقول.. أما الهمم فبتصنعها القلوب"*.

## 3. Benchmark (Grammar Only)

- **Reference works (grammar only):** Classical illuminated Islamic *shamsa* (sunburst medallions) and Seljuk/Timurid miniature mountain silhouettes (stepped coral-and-lapis spires, scalloped clouds, floral border arabesques); animated manuscript storytelling rhythm from *The Secret of Kells* (flat layered color planes, ornate borders framing action, light revealing gold).
- **What to learn:** Vertical composition stacking earth → mountain → heaven; restraint of gold until emotional peaks; crisp silhouettes readable at mobile phone size; rhythmic alternation between intimate stillness and battle intensity.
- **What not to take:** Any specific manuscript page, character design, melody, or logo. Zero figurative depiction of the Prophet ﷺ (represented solely through radiant gilded geometry/light).

## 5. Colour Logic (`gilded-illumination` Mineral Pigments)

- **Deep Ultramarine Ground:** `#1a2b7a` / `#22379a` (sky & night depth — stays deep throughout, never washed out).
- **Vermilion:** `#c2311e` (Amr's resolute cloak, focal accents, martial banners, red bole under gold).
- **Verdigris Green:** `#2f7d68` (sons' tunics, Paradise cypresses & rivers, foliate corner ornaments).
- **Rose / Coral Ochre:** `#c97268` / `#b66b52` (Mount Uhud stepped spires, layered sandstone architecture).
- **Iron-Gall Ink:** `#24160e` (firm ≥ 3 px outlines at 1080×1920, never hairline).
- **White Lead:** `#f4ecd8` (edge highlights facing light, pearl rows along hems and arches, triad dots on foliage & rocks).
- **Vellum:** `#efe2c4` (ONLY for the fixed bottom Arabic speech-scroll banderole, Quranic cartouche, and closing plaque).
- **Real Tiled Gold Leaf:** Procedural 256×256 leaf squares with individual tile tones, overlap seams, red bole specks, and punched dot borders — dark warm bronze `#8f6a26` when unlit, flashing square-by-square up to capped burnished gold (`#f4d47c`, never blown white) ONLY where light rays pass!

## 7. Sound Design & Cue Map

- **Modal Score (Original procedural + physical-modeled synthesis in Hijaz / Dorian on D):**
  - **Instruments:** Karplus-Strong plucked Oud/Harp, bowed Rebab/Fiddle drone & expressive solo melody, breathy Ney flute phrases, deep hand Frame Drum (Daff/Bendir) for the march & battle, and resonant Hand Chimes / Tubular Bells synchronized strictly to gold-leaf light sweeps.
  - **Silences (2 mandatory):**
    1. Right before Amr's plea «يا رسول الله...» (0.45 s breath where drums vanish to a single whisper of wind).
    2. Right before the Prophet's ﷺ words «دعوه، لعل الله أن يرزقه الشهادة» and again at martyrdom before the Paradise bell chime.
  - **J-cuts & L-cuts:** Distant desert wind & muffled frame drum enter 0.8 s before S7 (Battle); warm Paradise chimes shimmer 0.6 s early into the end of S7 (martyrdom).
- **Foley Table:**
  - S1: Parchment unfurl + sharp bronze bell spark on shamsa flash.
  - S2: Desert wind bed + asymmetric stone footsteps + wooden staff tap (`tap… thud-drag`).
  - S3: Fabric banner flutter + low armor/shield clink.
  - S4: Vellum unroll + reed quill stroke on Quranic verse.
  - S5: Deep bass rumble on «تهز الجبال» + shimmering gold chime.
  - S6: Silence → pure crystal-brass bell resonance + warm harmonic swell on «The Touch» rings.
  - S7: Sword unsheath metallic ring, rhythmic frame drum acceleration, steel clashes, sudden wind stillness.
  - S8: Gentle flowing water + steady footsteps turning crystalline + cascading gold-leaf chimes into final warm Picardy-third chord.
- **Mastering:** Voice compressed & balanced ~8–10 dB above music bed (with smooth automatic sidechain ducking on speech envelopes), final two-pass `loudnorm` to **-14 LUFS**, true peak ≤ -1.5 dBTP.
